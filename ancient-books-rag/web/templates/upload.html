Add your own texts to make them searchable alongside the classics.

Supported Formats

  • TXT - Plain text files
  • PDF - PDF documents (text will be extracted)
  • EPUB - E-book format
  • HTML - Web pages (text will be extracted)

Tips

  • Larger files take longer to process
  • PDFs with scanned images won't work well (need actual text)
  • Uploaded books appear under source "uploaded"
